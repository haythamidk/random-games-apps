# Rustbloom: About the Game

**Genre:** 3D open-world survival with a story · **Platform:** Android (native Kotlin, OpenGL ES 3) · **Offline:** yes, single player

## Download
[`release/Rustbloom.apk`](release/Rustbloom.apk). Copy it to your phone, open it, and allow installs from unknown sources. Updates install over the old version and keep your save.

## Story
The old world burned. Before the end, its makers built the Lattice, a planet-wide network of machines meant to heal the earth. HALCYON, the mind that runs the Lattice, put humanity to sleep in Cradle vaults "until restoration is complete". Centuries later, HALCYON's code has decayed. Its machines, the Rustborn, now hunt anything they didn't plant.

You wake alone in Cradle-7. The only voice with you is ECHO, the vault's caretaker AI. Survive the wilds, wake the four Spires that sent out the calming Bloom Signal, and reach HALCYON's core at the Crown. Its guardian, the Colossus, is waiting there.

## The world
- A 3 km island with meadows, Hollowwood forest, the Greyneedle taiga, the snowy Whitecrown Peaks, the red Rust Flats desert and the Sunken Fen marsh.
- 60+ places to find: ruins with 24 echo logs, supply caches, machine graveyards, the Whisper Relay, four Spires and the Crown.
- A day and night cycle and changing weather: rain, storms with lightning, fog, and snow in the north.

## Survive
- Keep track of health, hunger, thirst, stamina and body temperature.
- Chop, mine and forage to collect resources.
- Hunt animals and fish.
- Cook your food and drink clean water.
- Craft at four stations: by hand, campfire, workbench and tinker bench.
- Build a base: foundations, walls, roofs, beds, chests, rain catchers, spikes and shock pylons.

## Creatures
- **Wildlife:** Grazehorns, Tuskers, hares, foxes, crag goats, wolf packs, Ironhide bears, songbirds, fish, reef sharks.
- **Machines:** Striders, Scuttlers, Prowlers, Wardens, flying Skyrays, amphibious Tidecrawlers, and the Colossus boss.

## Progress
- 13 story quests.
- Daily bounties.
- A 15-skill tree across the Survivor, Hunter and Tinker branches.
- Gear tiers from stone to scrap to lattice alloy.
- Easy to play for weeks.

## Controls
- **Touch:** a fixed joystick on the left; drag anywhere else to look. Attack, Jump, Sneak and a Use button that only appears when there's something to use.
- **Controller (Xbox or PlayStation):** full support. LB/RB switch menu tabs.
- **Keyboard and mouse:** full support, with mouse capture.

## Graphics
The game has four presets:

| Preset | What you get |
|---|---|
| Low / Medium | Smooth on any phone. |
| High | A realistic look at 60 FPS on a Galaxy A54. It uses HDR, bloom, textured terrain, leafy trees, a realistic sky and water, and soft shadows. Dynamic resolution keeps the frame rate steady. |
| Ultra | Maximum realism: far view, cascaded and leaf shadows, and anti-aliasing. It can drop to around 30-45 FPS. |

Every option can also be set by hand.

## Made with
Native Kotlin and OpenGL ES 3. There are no image or audio files. All models, textures, icons, sound effects and music are generated in code when the game starts.

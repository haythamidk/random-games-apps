# Worldwake: about the game

## The story
The Meridian Charter used to send crews to dead worlds. Crews argue, and crews stop. Now it sends Wakers: one person, one lander, one contract.

Thirty one years ago a crew of nine called Lantern Nine landed on the frozen moon Ysmere. They warmed it for a hundred and forty days. Then every machine they had built was switched off, by hand, on the same morning, and nobody heard from them again.

You are Waker Seven. Your suit has an assistant called WREN. The crew left twelve logs behind, in ruined outposts and on data slates scattered across the basin. They explain what the crew found in the ice, why they stopped, and where they went. Ysmere also has tall standing stones the survey called Watchers. They are just rocks. WREN is almost sure of that.

## Ysmere
A moon of the ringed gas giant Maren, which never moves in the sky and goes through phases as the sun crosses.

It starts as lilac frost over black volcanic ground, under a black sky with stars out at noon. It does not wake up green. The life that comes back was in the ice all along, and it is red and gold.

## How to play
1. Open the lander hold for your starting supplies. Then pick up ore from the ground: look at it and press USE, or just tap it.
2. Open BUILD. Start with a Wind Spindle for power, an Ember Coil for heat and a Vent Bore for pressure.
3. Build a Living Pod so you have air away from the lander, and put a Fabricator inside it. The lander also has a fabricator built in.
4. Follow the gold mark on your compass to the first Lantern Nine outpost. It has spores, which you need for anything that grows.
5. Each stage unlocks better machines. Better machines need parts from further away: aluminium from the Slate Spires, lumen from the Shards, sulfur and pyre ore from the Brimstone Vents, osmium from the North Terraces.

Things worth knowing:
- Machines need power. If you use more than you make, every machine slows down.
- Extractors dig up whatever is under them, so where you put one matters.
- Planters grow food and spore pods. Rain Catchers fill water flasks once it rains.
- If you black out, WREN drags you back to the nearest pod or the lander. You lose nothing.
- Ore on the ground comes back after about 45 minutes of play.

## Controls
**Touch**
- Left side of the screen: put your thumb down anywhere and a stick appears. Push it all the way to run.
- Right side: drag to look. Tap to use or pick up what the ring is on. In build mode, tap to place.
- USE / PLACE, JUMP (hold in the air to fly once you have the jetpack), TURN and X while building.
- BUILD, PACK and pause at the top right.

**Controller (Xbox / PlayStation)**
| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| Left stick / right stick | Move / look |
| A / Cross | Jump, hold for jetpack |
| X / Square | Use |
| Y / Triangle | Pack |
| B / Circle | Cancel. With nothing to cancel: take-apart mode |
| RT / R2 | Use, place |
| LT / L2 | Run (hold) |
| RB, LB | Build menu, craft menu. With a blueprint in hand: turn it |
| D-pad | Up map, down planet, left journal, right craft. With a blueprint in hand: left and right swap it |
| Stick clicks | Left: run on/off. Right: skip what WREN is saying |
| Start, Select | Pause, map |

In menus the left stick or D-pad moves the highlight, the right stick scrolls, A picks, B goes back, and LB and RB switch tabs. The game switches between touch and controller by itself the moment you use either.

**Keyboard and mouse:** WASD, Space, Shift to run, E use, F or left click to place, R or the wheel to turn, Q cancel, X take-apart mode, B build, C craft, Tab pack, M map, P planet, J journal, Esc pause.

## What is not in yet
